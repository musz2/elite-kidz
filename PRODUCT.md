# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parents and guardians shopping for stylish, comfortable kidswear in Hyderabad. They need to browse a small curated collection, inspect each piece, choose a size, and contact Elite Kidz for availability and order guidance.

## Product Purpose

Elite Kidz is a lightweight e-commerce storefront for a curated kidswear collection. It helps customers discover products, filter and search the catalog, review product imagery and details, build a shopping bag, and continue the order conversation through WhatsApp.

## Brand Personality

Warm, considered, practical. The experience should feel welcoming to parents, calm to browse, and clear about the next step to purchase.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketplace clutter, aggressive discount-led merchandising, noisy children’s graphics, and unclear product actions. Do not hide size or order guidance behind ambiguous controls.

## Design Principles

- Make product discovery effortless.
- Let the supplied product photography lead.
- Keep ordering personal and transparent.
- Give every action clear feedback.
- Preserve a calm, trustworthy rhythm across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA basics: readable contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, meaningful labels and alt text, visible focus states, touch-friendly targets, and reduced-motion support.
